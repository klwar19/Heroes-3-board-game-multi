# PvE painted masters

Generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 2026-07-26.
These PNG files are illustration-only masters. The deterministic finishing
script `scripts/build-raid-dungeon-art.mjs` adds all typography, frames and
health-layer pips, then writes the project WebP assets.

Shared boss direction: richly painted late-1990s dark-fantasy strategy-game
oil illustration; vertical card portrait; centered readable silhouette;
generous safe margins; no text, frame, UI, numbers or watermark.

- `goblin_king.png`: bloated goblin tyrant on a scrap-shield throne, stolen
  crowns, oversized rusty cleaver, torchlit warren, sickly green/brass palette.
- `colossal_titan.png`: skinless colossus crushing a fortress wall at dusk,
  steam, ash and tiny fleeing figures.
- `abyss_kraken.png`: huge lidless eye, tentacles and a cracked galleon in a
  black-green storm sea.
- `calamity_dragon.png`: apocalyptic dragon, spread wings, torn burning sky,
  charged line of fire.
- `avatar_of_erebos.png`: crowned humanoid void, bent reality, kneeling
  monsters and soldiers averting their eyes.
- `minotaur_of_the_depths.png`: twin-axe minotaur warden, chained pillars,
  fogging breath and torchlit vault.
- `floor_wyrm.png`: pale blind segmented wyrm breaking through a spiral stair.
- `custom_boss.png`: deliberately unreadable many-limbed silhouette behind
  cracked enchanted glass and a summoning circle.
- `cyberdemon_prime.png`: original infernal cybernetic siege demon with rocket
  cannon in a ruined basalt fortress.
- `spider_overmind.png`: original exposed-brain mechanical spider commander
