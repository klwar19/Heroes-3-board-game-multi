# Chapter 1 layout: "Snow on the Road Home" (Haven) — for approval before writing

Current state: 25 scenes, most 3-18 lines, about 3,500 words, narrated in third person ("him").
The prologue is now about 18,000 words in first person. Chapter 1 is the emotional payoff of the
prologue, so it should be at least as full.

## Rules for this chapter
- **Same voice as the prologue**: narrator = Bin's first-person, present-tense inner voice.
  Style guide + `clannad-technique-notes.md` apply, with the varied rhythm rule: light-novel
  snark, Clannad back-and-forth and Tsukihime-style long introspective lines at quiet or eerie
  moments, mixed naturally (long when slow or deep, short when fast or funny). One quiet line per
  scene, open on a plain anchor line, close on a small image. Plain words. Adult jokes between adults.
- **Keep every mechanic**: all effects, flags, battles, quests, recruits and existing choices stay
  exactly as they are. Scenes are triggered by places and quests (see "Trigger"), so each one must
  work on its own and must not assume an order the game doesn't force.
- **No engine changes needed**: new material goes inside existing scenes. New choices only use
  existing effect kinds (record / karma / trait / points).
- **Hard line**: Mitia is 17 and Tuli is 6: nothing suggestive near them. Adult spice only with
  adults (Lysa flirts with everyone; Hilda, Mara and Frida are romance candidates, slow build).
- **Bin's past stays hidden**: it leaks only as smells, sounds and "I know this" moments. Nobody
  explains it. Garr knows something and does not say it.

## New running gags for Chapter 1
1. **Rude-candle coins** (existing counter): each find is ruder than the last. Jess keeps a tally
   and a growing grudge. Third coin: the candle seems to smirk.
2. **The town rumour chorus** (like Clannad's rumour boys): every scene, someone has a new theory
   of where Bin went. Eaten by a wolf -> married a sea witch -> became a god -> "owes money to a
   sea witch who is a god". It grows as the chapter goes on and pays off at dinner.
3. **Corvin's triplicate**: three rounds. The third time, Corvin hands Bin a form for filing forms,
   and Jess is quietly delighted.
