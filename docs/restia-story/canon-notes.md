# Canon notes (from the author's "new story example" doc)

The author's own world bible for Bin's Otherworld Chronicle. Treat as canon; the game script may simplify.

I. CORE PREMISE
Bin Chen, a broke, snarrky 20-year-old college student who supports his hospitalized younger sister Lily, is chosen by the trickster goddess Peri to become her Cosmic Jester — a cross-dimensional entertainer whose suffering, wit, and growth fuel a divine reality show. Armed with the Cosmic Jester System (CJS), Bin travels infinite worlds, completing quests, gaining power, and unraveling his mysterious past — while trying to save Lily, protect those he loves, and survive being the universe's favorite punchline.
Genre: RPG / D&D-style board game / story novel / role-play hybrid. Think manhwa progression fantasy meets tabletop campaign meets comedic novel.
Thematic Engine: Certain major character uses a mask (humor, control, coldness, perfection, "entertainment") to survive trauma. 
Tone: human and not try to act superior or know it all or dry and exhausted, just normal, a bit trolling and snarky and can be absurd sometimes, humorous, light hearted, somewhat pathetic but still hopeful.
References/Inspirations: D&D, Frosthaven (board game), Breath of Fire 2, Fallout, Solo Leveling, Gintama, Zombicide (board game), Cat System: The Emperor is a Cat Lover, Ultimate Scheming System manhwa, I'm an Evil God(我为邪帝) manhwa, cyberpunk genre, wuxia/xianxia cultivation fiction, Gothic Lolita aesthetic, isekai tropes (acknowledged and subverted), Monster girl quest paradox, Kujibiki Tokushou: Musou Harem-ken
II. ALL CHARACTERS (UNTIL NOW)
A. EARTH — MAIN CAST
BIN CHEN (Protagonist, 20)
Broke college student at Zhonghai University. Delivery worker. Deadpan humor, self-deprecating wit, surprisingly sharp tactical intelligence.
Raises Lily alone. Parents absent (circumstances TBD — plot hook).
Origin of his humor: At 17, confessed to Ling Ling with a cheap star-charm bracelet. She humiliated him publicly on camera. "Bracelet Boy" trended for a week. When he returned to school, he turned a bully's mockery into a joke and discovered: if you're already the joke, own the punchline.
SECRET (major, slow reveal across multiple arcs): Lived in Haven until age 15. Something happened involving Peri, powers, or choices he doesn't remember. Got sent to Earth with wiped memories. His childhood "world-building notebook" (frozen forests, guilds, a half-orc with a crossbow) is actually fragmented Haven memories leaking through.
Emotional core: Comedy is his armor. Underneath: kind, sincerity, stubborn hope.

Lily (~10) — Sharp, mischievous little sister. Hospitalized with a chronic illness. She's Bin's emotional core and motivation for everything. Currently stabilized by a healing crystal from Eos but needs more.
Luna Park ("Ice Queen") — Bin’ current crush. Sociology major who recognized Bin's tactical brilliance after the KFC video. Cold but not cruel (kuudere). Secret: she can see probabilities/futures, and a mysterious Organization exploits this ability using her family as leverage. She keeps distance from Bin to protect him, not out of disinterest.
Leo Zhang — Best friend. Loyal golden retriever energy. Always eating snacks. Films everything. Emotionally simple but deeply reliable. Knows nothing about the supernatural.
Meilin Wu — Childhood friend since age 7. The one who sees through everyone's masks. Named the pattern of Bin retreating into humor whenever he starts liking someone. Possibly has a deeper connection to the multiverse (left open).
Jake Hwang — Campus bully. Rich family, fragile ego. Can't win verbal fights against Bin so resorts to logistical sabotage. Uses Lily's illness as ammunition. Surface-level antagonist with potential for depth.


B. EARTH — ZHAO FAMILY / LING LING'S WORLD
Zhao Lingwei (Ling Ling) — The most complex antagonist. Met Bin at 14 at Old Zhou's bookstore, bonded over manga and wuxia. At 17, she almost said yes to his confession but saw her cousin Zhao Kang watching (a family loyalty test) and chose cruelty instead. The key insight: she knew kindness was possible and chose cruelty anyway — that's what makes her cut deeper than Jake. She kept the broken star charm. She's still conflicted.
Zhao Kang — Family enforcer, low-level cultivator, genuinely dangerous. Orchestrated the bracelet humiliation. Enjoys cruelty like fine wine.
Zhao Hengjun — Patriarch. Cold, strategic. Aware of "other planes." The family runs the Black Tortoise Sect, one of four ancient sects (Four Symbols) hidden in modern Chinese society. Their real power: a monopoly on Stardust trafficking — a multiverse resource from the Bazaar of Broken Chains that enhances cultivation. When Bin gets Bazaar access via CJS, he becomes their competitor/target.
 THE BLACK TORTOISE SECT
One of four ancient sects hidden within modern Chinese society (Four Symbols: Black Tortoise, Azure Dragon, White Tiger, Vermilion Bird).
Domain: Defense, endurance, water/ice techniques, information control.
Modern front: Zhao family's business empire. Sect operations woven into legitimate business.
Stardust trafficking: Stardust = multiverse resource from the Bazaar of Broken Chains that enhances cultivation. Black Tortoise has Earth's supply monopoly. Their real power and real crime.
When Bin gets Bazaar access via CJS, he becomes a competitor/target for the Zhaos.
C. THE GODDESS
Peri — Goddess of Narrative Convenience, Cosmic Observer, Professional Troll. Two forms: a petite adult woman in Gothic Lolita fashion (preferred, used with Bin; UPDATE 2026-09-30: she is an adult, not 14) and a terrifying divine aspect (rare, for cosmic-scale situations). She chose Bin as her Cosmic Jester because she sees herself in his humor-as-armor. Personality: Trolling, mocking, snarky — but with ancient depth peeking through cracks. 
Her motives are layered: surface = entertainment; middle = destabilizing her rival the Ethereal Judge; deep = her Lolita form is modeled on someone she lost — a previous mortal/Jester she cared about. Her "entertainment only" stance is her own mask, mirroring Bin's comedy armor. 
In Haven: Known in Norheim mythology as the Weaver of Fools. Garr met her decades ago. She told him something about Bin. He's been carrying the secret since.
E. COSMIC ENTITIES
THE ETHEREAL JUDGE (Antagonist — cosmic scale)
Enforcer of cosmic/narrative order. Peri's rival.
Opposes mortal interference. Believes Bin should be ordinary.
Will become a major threat as Bin's power grows.
May have been responsible for the previous Jester's fate.
The Cosmic Jester System (CJS)
A cross-dimensional power system that functions like an RPG interface with manhwa troll energy. Bin earns Jester Points (JP) through quests, creativity, comedic timing, trolling, and spending. He can buy skills, items, and upgrades from the Cosmic Jester Shop. Skills carry between worlds but with reduced/scaled effect. Combat uses a D&D-style system with SPECIAL stats, elemental affinities, dice rolls, damage resistance, and QTE minigames (Rubik's Cube, Jenga, etc.). Each world has unique additional combat/gameplay mechanic. 
The Multiverse Structure
Haven is the first and also main world — a vast multi-continent high fantasy setting. Bin's party there: Garr (veteran hunter, adoptive father, holds a secret from Peri), Bowy (half-orc/half-boar marksman, Bin's adopted brother, talks normally), and Mitia (appears human, secretly has a hidden bloodline/Fae heritage, the emotional heart of the group). They're F-rank adventurers in Frostbitten, a frozen northern settlement.
Other worlds include: Zombie Apocalypse, Cyberpunk Neo-Kowloon, Wuxia Realms, and more TBD. The Bazaar of Broken Chains is a shared neutral hub for cross-world trade. Bin also unlocks Pocket Haven — a customizable pocket dimension for crafting, recruiting, and farming.

PLOT SUMMARY — EARTH PROLOGUE THROUGH TUTORIAL
The Backstory (Age 14–17)
14: Bin meets Ling Ling at Old Zhou's bookstore. They bond over manga, wuxia, cultivation fiction. Saturday regulars. Shared passion for fantasy world-building. Bin shows her his private notebook (frozen forests, guilds, a half-orc with a crossbow — actually fragmented Haven memories). She doesn't laugh. She looks at the drawing of an armored girl on an ice cliff for a long time.
15: Ling Ling drifts. Family pulls her in. Zhao Kang appears. Saturdays thin. She becomes polished and distant. Old Zhou notices: "She used to laugh. Now she performs." Meilin (who met Ling Ling once) warns Bin: "She looks at you like she's deciding whether to keep the book or put it back."
16: Saturdays stop entirely. Ling Ling texts in single words. Leo arrives — hungry, loyal, simple. Becomes Bin's anchor. Meilin watches from the side, waiting for the crash she can see coming.
17: Bin confesses. Star-charm bracelet. Ling Ling almost says yes, sees Zhao Kang watching, chooses cruelty. Public humiliation. "Bracelet Boy" trends. Star charm breaks, rolls under a car. Bin doesn't go to school for 3 days. Leo shows up with a duck. Meilin shows up with a key and silence. On return, Bin turns a bully's locker prank into a joke: "You misspelled 'biggest.' Two G's." The hallway laughs WITH him. The Ling Ling Rule is born: If you're already the joke, own the punchline.
Present Day — Campus Life (Chapters 0.2–0.4)
Jake's pattern established: Escalating from verbal jabs (which Bin wins) to logistical sabotage (scooter flats, stolen homework). Uses Lily's illness as ammunition. Bin handles him through counter-trolling — making bullying exhausting by reviewing insults like a comedy critic. Jake can't win because Bin refuses to be a victim.
Bin's daily life: 6:30 AM noodles → delivery shifts → classes → lunch with Leo and Meilin → more deliveries → hospital visit to Lily → home → collapse. Between deliveries, he narrates his life like a documentary: "Here we see the delivery boy in his natural habitat — the elevator that smells like regret."
First Luna encounter: Bin nearly crashes into Luna during a delivery. Word-vomits. Flees. That night, Lily detects the crush immediately. "Your ears are red and you've been smiling at your phone."
The Viral Spiral (Chapters 1–2)
KFC Incident: Streamer ChadThunderLive tries to humiliate Bin on camera. Bin delivers deadpan pickup lines and self-deprecating philosophy. Goes viral as "The Durian Prince."
Luna's reaction: "Strategically brilliant. You took his platform." Not warmth — recognition. First crack.
Lily's fan club: Shows the video to the entire pediatric ward. Does impressions of Bin. Chemo kid asks for a pickup line. Bin: "The best pickup line is asking someone how their day was. And listening." Kid: "That's not funny." Bin: "No. But it works."
Jake's retaliation: Uses Lily's illness in a public confrontation. Bin fires back. Jake charges. Bin runs. Trash Can Incident: Bin flips into a wheeled trash can in front of the science building. Luna walks out. Looks down at him. "Is this another dating strategy? Because it's certainly memorable."
The Sky Rant: Bin, garbage-covered, yells at the universe. Demands answers. Breaks through the comedy armor: "Just. Lily. Let her get better. That's it. That's the whole thing." The sky does nothing. No thunder. No intervention. Just silence and phones recording.
Bin walks home. Showers 45 minutes. Crashes into bed at 9:47 PM.
The Goddess (Chapter 3)
Bin goes back to find a girl sitting cross-legged on his bedroom floor, scrolling through his phone. Peri appears as a petite adult woman in Gothic Lolita style (updated 2026-09-30: adult, not 14)—black dress with cosmic patterns, blue highlights like nebulae, platform boots, twin tails floating in an impossible breeze. She holds a phone made of starlight.. Roasts his search history, his passcode (1234), his life. Reveals she's been watching since the shopping cart incident (which she caused). Finds his hidden Ling Ling photos — comments that "she's going to be a problem."
Peri reveals she's the Goddess of Narrative Convenience, Cosmic Observer, and Professional Troll. She's been watching him for entertainment value. She offers him a deal—not as a "champion" (too noble and boring), but as her Jester. Bin resists — his humor-armor kicks in. When Peri mentions Lily, Bin drops the comedy: "Don't use Lily. That's the one thing you don't joke about." Peri goes still — for one second, the mask slips. "I'm not joking. That's the most serious thing I've heard in a very long time." Then the grin returns.
Peri offers a free trial — 3 days, one world, no contract. "Smart jesters read the fine print." Mentions "the last twelve candidates" casually (what happened to them = major mystery). Kicks Bin through a portal before he can ask more questions.
Tutorial World — Eos (Chapter 4)
Setting: Eos is a constructed pocket dimension Peri maintains for candidate testing. Classic JRPG starting zone — rolling hills, village, forest, cave dungeon. Everything is slightly too perfect. It's a sandbox, and it feels like one.
The next day, Bin is absentminded—distracted, staring into space, responding slowly. His friends notice.
Leo (mid-bite into a jianbing): "Bro. You okay? You've been staring at that wall for five minutes."
Meilin (eyes narrowed): "You're doing the thing where your brain leaves your body. What happened?"
Bin mumbles something about not sleeping well.
Then Meilin drops a bombshell:
"Also, weird question—did something happen with Luna? She's been acting even stranger than usual. I saw her watching you across the quad. Like, really watching. Not her normal ice-queen glare. Something different."
Bin's heart skips. Luna? Watching him?
He doesn't know that Luna's organization has noticed him. That she's trying to warn him without getting close. That she's terrified of what they'll do if they think he matters.
Chapter 5: The Contract
That night, Peri appears again. Bin agrees to become her Jester—officially. He signs the Cosmic Jester System (CJS) contract.
Terms:
Travel between worlds, complete narratively significant quests
Generate entertaining content (his suffering is a feature, not a bug)
Earn Jester Points (JP) for quests, creativity, and comedic timing
Spend JP in the Cosmic Jester Shop for skills, items, upgrades
Skills can carry to other worlds but with reduced effect (scaling)
Not die permanently (at least not without dramatic impact)
In exchange: power to eventually save Lily and help those he cares about
Peri opens the Haven portal. Cold air. Pine and snow and woodsmoke. Bin hears distant sounds — a crossbow string, a fire, someone saying his name.
"This feels like going back," Bin says.
"Interesting choice of words," Peri says. Too lightly. "Most people say 'going forward.'"
She kicks him through.

WORLD 01: HAVEN - DETAILED SETUP
Timeline Context
Two months ago in Haven time: Bin disappeared mysteriously. No warning, no trace. He simply vanished from Frostbitten, leaving behind Garr, Bowy, and Mitia—who have been searching, grieving, and hoping ever since.
Now: Bin reappears just outside Frostbitten, confused and memory-fogged. Two months have passed for them. For Bin, it's been... complicated.
The Party
Garr – Veteran hunter, adoptive father figure. Late 50s, weathered, one eye blind (milky white), the other sharp as a blade. Raised three orphans in his small hut. Knows more than he says—including something about the goddess Peri that he's never revealed. When Bin reappears, Garr's reaction is controlled, but his one good eye holds depths of relief and suspicion. He's been expecting something like this. Not Bin's return specifically, but something. His secret with the goddess remains hidden—for now.
Bowy – Orc/boar hybrid (half-boon, half-orc). Built like a brick wall covered in coarse fur, with tusks curving from his lower jaw. Carries an enormous crossbow named "Thunder" that he built himself. Talks normally—deep, rumbling voice, gruff but warm. Not a man of many words, but when he speaks, it counts. He's Bin's adopted brother in every way that matters. When Bin reappears, Bowy punches him in the shoulder (affectionate), then hugs him (aggressive), then demands to know where the hell he's been.
Mitia – Appears completely human. Seventeen now, shy but warming up around family. Long dark hair, brown eyes, quiet smile. She's the heart of the group—the one who keeps everyone fed, who remembers birthdays, who cries when Bin returns and tries to pretend she didn't. Secret: She's the heir to something. A bloodline. A legacy. She doesn't know the full truth yet—only that she's different, that there are expectations she doesn't understand, that her parents (who died when she was young) weren't ordinary. The signs are subtle: occasional dreams of places she's never seen, a strange calmness around certain magics, the way animals sometimes... listen to her. Garr knows more than he's told her. Bin's return will accelerate things.
Bin – Called Bin here too (no name change). Twenty years old (physically), though his memories of Haven are fragmented. He knows he lived here until 15, knows these people are family, but the details are hazy. His secret is not revealed any time soon. Something happened seven years ago. Something that got him sent to Earth with no memories. Something involving Peri, or powers, or choices he doesn't remember making. It will emerge slowly, over multiple arcs, as pieces of the puzzle click into place.
Frostbitten Setting
A small northern settlement clinging to survival. Surrounded by the Frostwood—a massive frozen forest full of wolves, bandits, ruins, and mysteries. The cold is relentless. Everyone wears furs. Breath freezes. Fire is life.
Key locations:
Garr's Hut – Small, warm, smells of woodsmoke and dried meat
The Adventurer's Guild – Central hub, run by Guild Master Voren (details below)
The Frosted Mug – Tavern/inn, warm food and colder ale
Ironhand Forge – Blacksmith, run by Hilda Ironhand
Old Temple Ruins – North in Frostwood, current quest location
Guild Introduction Scene
Bin, Garr, Bowy, and Mitia walk into the guild hall together. It's bustling—adventurers of various ranks, staff behind counters, notice boards covered in quests.
Guild Staff:
Lysa – Front desk receptionist, early 20s, cheerful, excellent at remembering names, flirts with everyone indiscriminately. "Bin! You're back! Wait—you're back back? Like, not dead back? Oh thank the gods, Garr's been moping for two months."
Corvin – Senior clerk, 40s, perpetually tired, processes paperwork with the enthusiasm of someone who's given up. "Another party for F-rank registration. Wonderful. Just what I needed. Please fill out these forms in triplicate. Yes, all of them. No, I don't make the rules."
Tessa – Quest assigner, mid-30s, no-nonsense, sharp eyes. She sizes up returning adventurers instantly. "You disappeared for two months. That's usually fatal. You look okay. Mostly. Take something easy first." (She assigns the mushroom gathering quest as a warm-up)
Other Adventurers Present:
Dain – C-rank fighter, cocky, scar on his cheek, leans against the counter making loud comments. "Well well, the ghost returns. Figured you'd frozen to death in a ditch somewhere."
Mara – D-rank mage, patient, rolls her eyes at Dain. "Ignore him. He's just jealous you got a dramatic disappearance and he's never interesting."
Kael – E-rank rogue, young, nervous, clearly new. Stares at Bin with wide eyes. "You're the one who came back? Everyone talks about you. Well, not everyone. Some people. I heard stories."
The Brinna Twins – F-rank like Bin's group, sisters (Ella and Nessa), friendly rivals. "You owe us three silver from before you vanished. We didn't forget."
The guild feels alive—full of personalities, rivalries, friendships. It's not just a backdrop; it's a community.
