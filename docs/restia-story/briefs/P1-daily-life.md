## Where it sits
After the bookstore flashback (Bin at 14-17). Before the KFC livestream. Present day, Zhonghai.
Bin Chen, 20: delivery rider, part-time student, looks after his sick little sister Lily alone.
Broke. Backgrounds: `earthApartment`, then `earthCampus`, then `earthHospital` at night.

## Beats (play each one out; don't summarize)
1. **6:30 AM.** Instant noodles. Bin narrates his own life like a nature documentary:
   "Here we see the delivery boy in his natural habitat..." Bank balance (dire). A text from Lily
   with far too many emojis.
2. **Jake's sabotage.** Flat scooter tyre, again. A sticky note in Jake's handwriting. Bin reviews
   the prank like a film critic ("Two stars. Derivative. The sticky note is a nice touch.").
   The old repairman at the stall knows him by name ("Third time this month. Enemies or bad luck?"
   "Why not both.").
3. **The Gym Bro delivery** (the user's idea; keep the core joke). Delivering a protein shake to the
   university gym. A huge gym bro (speaker `gymBro`) bumps him to show off to a girl: "Watch where
   you're going, twig! I could bench-press you!" Bin looks him over with zero fear, purely on
   logistics. Player **choice** of three replies (use label + goto):
   - Zombie logistics: "You could. Excellent deltoids. In a zombie apocalypse you'd be an incredible
     frontline distraction. Your protein content alone would keep the horde busy ten minutes.
     Thank you for your sacrifice."
   - One about the shake (you invent).
   - A sincere-but-baffling compliment (you invent).
   All three end the same: Bin hands over the shake and leaves; the gym bro can't tell if he was
   complimented, threatened or academically analyzed; the girl bursts out laughing.
4. **Lunch with Leo and Meilin.** Leo is eating (always) and filming. Meilin notices Bin hasn't
   eaten; food quietly migrates onto his tray without anyone mentioning it.
5. **First Luna encounter.** Rushing a delivery, Bin nearly crashes into Luna Park. He word-vomits
   (the weather, pens, his own name twice). She says very little, precisely ("You said your name
   twice." / "...Yes."). He flees. His inner monologue melts down.
6. **Night, hospital.** Lily clocks it in 30 seconds: "Your ears are red and you've been smiling at
   your phone." She interrogates him like a detective; every deflection fails. End quiet: she asks
   if he's eating properly, he lies, she knows, neither says it. Then she steals his last biscuit.

Bin's inner monologue is the narrator (first person, present tense).
