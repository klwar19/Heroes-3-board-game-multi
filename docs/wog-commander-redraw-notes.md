# WOG Commander Redraw Notes

Source used for the nine original WOG commanders:
<http://www.heroesofmightandmagic.com/wakeofgods/comm2.shtml>

Art-direction target for the 2026-07-05 redraw:
- Original board-game unit-card feel, especially the Castle Crusaders and Archangels crops in `scripts/commander-art/reference/`.
- Pro artistic HD sketch finish: visible graphite/ink structure, refined gouache/acrylic color, muted printed-card grain.
- Avoid heavy oil-paint styling, AI gloss, plastic 3D surfaces, fake signatures, card frames, text, numbers, icons, or watermarks baked into the art.
- Keep one readable full-body commander figure with enough padding for the 504x658 art window used by `scripts/build-commander-cards.mjs`.

WoG concept cues used:
- Paladin: divine defender of justice, holy avenger, Wise, Cure.
- Hierophant: mystical natural healer, First Aid Master, Shield.
- Temple Guardian: strong-willed titan kin, mana channeler, Precision.
- Succubus: charming Inferno commander, neutral-stack charm, Fire Shield.
- Brute: fearless Dungeon fighter, Bloodlust, Soul Reformer.
- Soul Eater: undead essence-drinker, Animate Dead.
- Ogre Leader: resourceful Stronghold ballista commander, Stone Skin.
- Shaman: primal Fortress totem magic, Superior Combat, Haste.
- Astral Spirit: astral-plane elemental, pacifist visions, Counterstrike.
- Corsair, Engineer, and Frost Warlord are original additions for Cove, Factory, and Bulwark.
