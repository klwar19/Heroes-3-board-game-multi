# Raid Bosses & Dungeon — art direction (placeholder → final)

Everything under `public/assets/bosses/` is **PROCEDURAL PLACEHOLDER art** —
committed, functional, deliberately upgradeable. It is generated by
`scripts/build-raid-dungeon-art.mjs` (deterministic SVG → sharp → webp;
re-running reproduces identical files). This doc is the instruction sheet for
the future AI/artist pass that replaces them with painted art.

## Target style

Match the repo's late-90s HoMM3 board-game aesthetic: dark-fantasy oil-painting
look, warm rim light, ornate gold double frame with corner flourishes, a name
plate at the top (name + italic subtitle) and the HEALTH LAYERS pip row at the
bottom. The final faces should be full illustrated monster portraits INSIDE the
frame — keep the name plate and the layer pips (they are functional reads:
players count bars off the card).

## Per-boss portrait briefs (prompts for an image-gen pass)

- **Goblin King** — a bloated goblin tyrant slouched on a throne of scrap
  shields and stolen crowns, torchlit warren behind, a rusty oversized cleaver
  across his knees; sickly green palette, mocking grin.
- **Colossal Titan** — a skinless colossus looming OVER a fortress wall at
  dusk, steam rising off exposed muscle, one hand crushing the parapet; ash
  grey palette, tiny fleeing silhouettes for scale.
- **Abyss Kraken** — an abyssal kraken dragging a cracked galleon under a
  black-green sea, one huge lidless eye catching lantern light, tentacles
  breaking the surface in arcs; deep teal palette.
- **Calamity Dragon** — a cataclysmic dragon silhouetted against a torn,
  burning sky, wings spread over a scorched valley, breath charging as a line
  of fire; ember red/orange palette.
- **Avatar of Erebos** — a walking god of darkness: a towering humanoid void
  wearing a crown of black light, reality bending around its steps, worshipful
  monsters kneeling at its feet; void purple palette. Its FEAR should read in
  the composition (soldiers averting their eyes).
- **Minotaur of the Depths** — a hulking minotaur in torchlit dungeon vaults,
  chained pillars behind, twin axes, breath fogging; rust/bronze palette.
- **The Floor Wyrm** — a pale segmented wyrm coiled up THROUGH a broken spiral
  staircase, jaws splitting the stonework, blind head tracking by sound;
  bone-white/grey palette.
- **Custom Boss (shared face)** — keep it ABSTRACT: a shadowed silhouette
  behind cracked glass / a summoning circle, "shape unknown" — it stands in
  for any designer-made monster, so no specific anatomy.

Map hexes:
- **Rift Lair** — the sky cracked open above a blasted field: a jagged
  purple-white rift star, red afterglow rings, black spike debris at the base.
  Composition centered (it is hex-clipped with cover fit, ~8% safe margin).
- **Dungeon Gate** — a massive stone arch set into rock, keystone blocks,
  pitch-black interior with a faintly lit descending stair, two torch glows.

## Replacement procedure

1. Generate/paint the replacement at the SAME path and SAME dimensions
   (743×1040 for faces, 512×512 for hexes), webp.
2. `git add public/assets/bosses/...` and commit — the CDN sync workflow
   (`.github/workflows/sync-media-r2.yml`) uploads on push and purges cache on
   `main`. **Never hand-edit the R2 bucket** (manual fallback:
   `npm run sync:assets`).
3. If hand-painted masters are produced, store them under
   `scripts/anime-art/raw/bosses/` with prompt provenance noted beside each
   file (the repo's raw-masters convention), and retire the corresponding
   procedural generation from `scripts/build-raid-dungeon-art.mjs` so a re-run
   cannot clobber real art.
4. Tests only assert the files EXIST at these paths (never that they are
   final) — a swap needs no test change.
