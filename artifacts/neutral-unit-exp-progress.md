# All neutral-unit experience progressions

Generated from the current engine data. This covers every non-summon unit definition that has a recruitable Neutral side.

## Progression rules

- Recruited neutral cards use normal Unit Experience and can reach all four ranks.
- XP thresholds: Bronze 5/9/13/17; Silver 6/10/15/20; Gold and Azure 8/13/19/25.
- Neutral Rank-Up field guards use round-based mirrored XP and are capped at Elite (rank 3): Bronze rounds 3/5/8; Silver 6/8/12; Gold/Azure 6/10/14.
- Creature-bank defenders with Neutral Rank-Up: Far rounds 4/6/9; Near rounds 6/8/12.
- Dragon Hunt defenders at Dragon Utopia are at least Seasoned (rank 1) when Unit Experience is active; Neutral Rank-Up can raise them further according to its round schedule.
