## Changes

- Market routing and market opening now share the same concrete trade/purchase assessment. Arrived markets and markets already used this round stop being march targets. Shops cannot masquerade as free pickup detours. Non-urgent trading yields to reachable map work, including longer marches.
- FAR-supply navigation now uses the public supply count consistently, so the AI's redacted view does not hide available expansion routes.
- Mines of every resource type and settlements at difficulty 2 or higher reserve up to two paid combat continuations, capped by refreshed movement minus entry cost. Automatic wins, free continuations, and unlimited-combat locations retain their exemptions. Pickup detours must preserve next-turn approach plus this reserve.
- Detailed rotation scoring applies to FAR and NEAR tiles. It compares real routes through the map's walls and guard stops, travel distance, useful rewards, and movement available for battle. Accessible rewards that are too strong today still contribute a future-return route value, without allowing an unsafe fight.
- Combat focus scoring only counts allies with an actual immediate legal attack, excludes paralyzed/spent allies, and uses Pack removal durability. PvP repositioning and defending consider legal enemy move-and-attack replies; Wait no longer treats shooters or paralyzed enemies as melee bait.
