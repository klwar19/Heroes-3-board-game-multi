# PvP ranked audit + "what could be done better" — 2026-09-15

**Scope:** all 34 ranked replays (`room-room-*`), 25 player-vs-player fights across 18
games (19 decisive, 6 pre-deploy concedes). Read-only reconstruction; **no engine
run, no code change.** Data packs in scratchpad (`fight-packs.txt`, `pvp-metrics.txt`).

## How to read this report (method)

- **I judge the POSITION on its merits, not who won.** Players quit / lose for
  reasons outside their play (rage-quit, disconnect, misclick, real life). A
  give-up is **not** proof the board was lost. Where I say "better line", it is me
  putting myself in that seat and reasoning the play — not "the winner did X".
- **Two kinds of statements, labelled:**
  - **[DOCTRINE]** = a rule you have confirmed. I treat it as final.
  - **[IDEA]** = my own suggestion. **It may be wrong — it's here for you to fix.**
- **[CODE]** = what the current AI already does, quoted so you can check I'm not
  about to break something that's already right.

Confirmed doctrines so far:
- **[DOCTRINE] Spells → gold body only.** PvP damage spells (Magic Arrow, Lightning,
  Implosion, Chain Lightning, etc.) chip the enemy **gold lvl-7 body (2–3 def)**.
  **NEVER bronze** — not even a bronze shooter. Spells ignore Defense, so the spell
  is the only thing that punches an armored gold stack; killing a bronze wastes it.
- **[DOCTRINE] Chip is fine even against healing.** Don't be scared off the gold
  body by their First Aid Tent / heal. Counter the heal with **timing + focus**:
  concentrate damage so a stack drops faster than they heal per round.
- **[DOCTRINE] Magic Arrow targeting** (prior ruling): focuses **Def-2 units**
  (Gorgon/Dendroid/Mummy), not shooters.
- **[DOCTRINE] Don't blame losses on decisions.** Good play still loses.

## 1. The strategic layer decides most fights (before a single combat card is played)

PvP is a **late-game** event: initiation rounds were 6–18, **median round 10**. By
the time two armies meet, the buildup (economy → gold ladder → hero level → war
machines → army synergy) has usually already set the result. Fight-outcome and
game-outcome agreed in 18 of 23 decisive fights, so the meeting is the hinge — but
the *hinge is loaded during rounds 1–N*.

What actually carries a winning position (from the 19 decisive fights, judged on
board state not final result):

| Lever | Evidence | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold bodies present** | Every dominant army had gold/azure stacks (dc1o0g p1: 5 Archangels+4 Champions; b7gyqi p4; 910btc p1) | Reaching a gold army by ~R7 is the single biggest edge. |
| **Hero level** | Higher-level seat held the better position in ~10/19 (expert effects, commander stat points, spell slots) | Level = spell power + commander grades + ability searches. |
| **War machine (First Aid Tent)** | Present on the stronger side in 8 fights; it heals **every round** (dc1o0g, dukynw, 62mh6l, b7gyqi, vw15ix) | Sustained per-round heal is a huge combat multiplier; cheap (6 gold). |
| **Army SYNERGY, not raw tier** | 910btc: a **silver** Necropolis army (Liches + Vampires + commander heal) ground down a **gold+azure** army | Tier count is not strength. Kits win. See §3. |

**[IDEA — your call]** The largest "what could be done better to win" across these
games is **not tactical** — it is reaching a gold body + a First Aid Tent + a hero
level or two earlier. Several losing positions were bronze/silver armies arriving at
a round-10+ fight against a gold army (dc1o0g p2, mnrtc8 p2, cpgbmq R12 p1). Those
seats' *combat* play was often fine; the *buildup* is where the game was decided.
(This aligns with the committed gold-ladder / city-hall-timing economy rules.)

## 2. Spell use — chip the gold body, beat the heal with timing + focus

### 2.1 What the code does today [CODE]

`scoreDamageEffect` ([card-policy.ts:484](../src/engine/computer/card-policy.ts#L484)):
- Uses **printed, Defense-ignoring** damage (comment on line 538 explicitly warns
  against the old `attack−defense` guess that "steered every cast at the cheapest
  chaff instead of the real threat"). ✅ correct direction.
- `armorLeverage` (line 575): **more spell value the higher the target's defense**,
  plus a bonus when melee deals it zero. ✅ pushes toward armored bodies.
- **BUT**: a **lethal** hit gets `+50 (+30 unacted, +25 if def≥2)`; a non-lethal
  chip gets only the threat/armor terms. So a spell that **kills a bronze** can
  outscore **chipping a gold** body.

Worked numbers (typical):
- Spell that kills a bronze (def 1): `threat 10 + full-frac 40 + lethal 50 + unacted 30 ≈ 130`.
- Spell chipping a gold Archangel (def 3, ~30% hp): `threat 60(cap) + 12 + armor 9 ≈ 81`.
- → **The AI currently prefers the bronze kill.**

### 2.2 The mistake this is [DOCTRINE says fix]

You ruled: **never bronze, always the gold body.** So the code above is a mistake to
flag: the AI should chip the gold lvl-7 body and **not** spend the spell killing a
bronze, even a "lethal, unacted, def≥2" bronze. The `+50/+30/+25` lethal terms are
exactly what let a bronze kill jump the gold chip.

**[IDEA — candidate fix, not coded]** In a **player (PvP)** combat, make the damage-
spell target order **gold/azure ≫ silver ≫ bronze**, so the highest-tier reachable
enemy body is always the spell target regardless of lethality on a lower tier. Keep
the neutral-fight logic untouched (guards are scripted; there the lethal/armor logic
is fine). I would gate this on `combat.context.kind === "player"` and add a
mutation-checked test: *a gold body present ⇒ the spell scores the gold body above a
lethal bronze kill.* **Your call on whether "always gold, ignore lethal-bronze" is
exactly right, or whether silver-when-no-gold is the fallback.**

### 2.3 The heal counter — timing + focus (dc1o0g, played out from p2's seat)

Position (dc1o0g R14): p2 (bronze/silver Tower-ish: 4 Gremlins, 2 Magi shooters,
Genies, Iron Golems, temple_guardian) vs p1 (**5 Archangels + 4 Champions**, paladin,
**First Aid Tent** + rion heal specialties). p1 healed the Archangels **+1 (tent),
+1/+2/+1 (rion)** in round 1 alone.

p2's actual line: Lightning → Archangels, Lightning → Archangels, melee chips into
Archangels, Implosion → Archangels. **Correct target (the gold body).** p2 still lost.

**Was chipping wrong? No** — [DOCTRINE]. **The better line is timing + focus:** p2's
damage was spread as thin chips across the Archangel wall while p1 healed ~5/round.
To beat a ~5/round heal you must **pile a whole round's damage onto ONE Archangel
stack** and remove it in that round (a dead stack can't be healed), then move to the
next — instead of leaving several Archangels alive-but-chipped for the tent/rion to
top back up. p2 actually *did* remove one Archangel R1, which is the right idea; the
improvement is to do that **every** round with concentrated fire rather than spread.

**[IDEA — candidate]** When the enemy has a per-round heal source (First Aid Tent,
heal ability/specialty, Vampire drain), bias focus-fire even harder toward
**finishing one stack this round** (the code's `armyCanFinish` / `FOCUS_FINISH_BONUS`
already exists — this would *raise* its weight when enemy heal-per-round is detected).
Not coded; flagged.

### 2.4 Anti-magic

Winners and losers both carried `anti_magic` / `protection_from_*` reactions. A spell
into an anti-magic'd target is wasted. **[IDEA]** the spell scorer does not currently
check whether the target can react with anti-magic / spell-ward (it respects printed
`previewSpellDamage` wards but not a held reaction). Low confidence this is worth the
complexity (reactions are hidden information) — flagging honestly.

## 3. Focus fire & target priority — kill the damage ENGINE, one stack at a time

### 3.1 910btc played out from p1's seat (the weaker-looking army won — why, and the better line)

Position (910btc R18): p1 = **6 Archangels (gold) + 6 Rust Dragons (azure) + 6
Halberdiers**, paladin, lvl 7. p2 = **3 Liches (silver shooters) + 3 Vampires
(silver) + Wraiths**, lvl 6, commander that heals +2/round. On tier count p1 is far
ahead. p2 won.

Why p2's kit is strong (regardless of the result): **Liches are AoE shooters**
(death-cloud splashes adjacent), **Vampires drain life and take no retaliation**, and
the **commander heals +2 every round**. So p2 out-sustains and punishes clustering.

The better line for p1 (me in the seat): **the three Liches are the entire p2 damage
engine — delete them turn 1.** p1 has **azure Rust Dragon FLIERS** that can cross the
board and land on the back-row Liches immediately. p1's actual R1 line spread across
Liches with Rust Dragons + Archangels + commander + an Implosion, but did **not**
remove the Lich line fast; by R3 only some Liches were gone and the Vampires + heal
had done their work. Concentrate **all** flier reach on the Liches R1–R2 (they have
def 1 — trivial for gold/azure melee), accept that Halberdiers screen, and the fight
flips. Also: p1 **clustered** its gold stacks (many at C1/C2/D1/D2) into Lich AoE.

Lessons (merit-based, not "p2 won so Necropolis is better"):
- **[IDEA] Kill the damage engine first**: enemy shooters / AoE units / output-ability
  stacks (Liches, Magi, Manticores, Behemoths) before trading bodies. The code already
  has `RANGED_TARGET_BONUS (+18)`, `CASTER_TARGET_BONUS (+14)`, `hasOutputAbility (+12)`
  — this is largely covered [CODE]. The gap is **reach**: using fliers to *get to* the
  backline shooters, not just preferring them when already adjacent.
- **[IDEA] Don't cluster vs AoE** (see §4).

### 3.2 One stack per round (heal counter, §2.3) — general focus rule

Across the fights, the side that **removed whole stacks** (rather than leaving many
chipped) controlled the tempo. Concentrating a round's damage to finish a stack:
(a) removes a whole activation of enemy output, (b) can't be undone by heal. The code
rewards this (`FOCUS_FINISH_BONUS`, `armyCanFinish`, focus march) [CODE] — good; the
only [IDEA] is to lean on it harder when enemy heal is present (§2.3).

### 3.3 The commander is not a normal target

Several seats poked the enemy commander with a non-lethal hit and lost the attacker to
retaliation (a commander's removal is costly, so it retaliates hard). The code already
scores a non-lethal commander poke that dies on retaliation **below END_ACTIVATION**
(`COMMANDER_RETALIATION_DEATH_SCORE = 350`) [CODE]. ✅ covered. **Do** hit the
commander when it's lethal or when it's the last threat — the code allows that.

## 4. Formation — screen shooters, protect the gold body, and DON'T over-cluster

### 4.1 What's already right [CODE]

`formationFitScore` ([combat-policy.ts:551](../src/engine/computer/combat-policy.ts#L551)):
shooters to the **back row + corners**, melee **screen** in front, column diversity
(penalty for 3+ in one file), commander/gold bodies to safer cells, flyer reserve,
exposure penalty on gold/azure. The Castle Griffin/Halberdier pairing is a user ruling
and is honored. This matches good human deployment in the packs (shooters back,
screens up).

### 4.2 The one gap I see: AoE clustering

Formation today optimizes single-target screening and melee-collapse exposure
(`positionalExposurePenalty`), but it does **not** avoid **clustering into enemy AoE**
(Lich death-cloud, Magog fireball splash, dragon breath line, Chain Lightning). In
910btc p1's gold stacks sat adjacent in a 2×2 block — ideal Lich food.

**[IDEA — candidate]** When the enemy army contains an AoE/splash source, add a
placement/movement penalty for putting 2+ of your own valuable stacks orthogonally
adjacent (spread the gold bodies so one death-cloud / splash hits at most one). Low-to-
medium confidence — needs care not to break the screen (screening *requires*
adjacency of a cheap melee body to the shooter; the penalty must apply to *valuable*
stacks clustering, not to a chaff screen). Flagging honestly for your ruling.

## 5. Defend / move / protect / block

**[CODE] Already implemented and matching good play:**
- **Defend the high-value body** under lethal incoming when it's worth saving
  (`combat.defend-high-value`, +50; surround detection) — and never defend twice.
- **Screen friendly shooters**; **block flying threats' landing** on your shooters
  (orthogonal-neighbor block, +18); step between enemy and your ranged.
- **Ranged discipline**: strong penalty for a shooter walking adjacent to melee;
  post-shot disengage; escape a lethal reply (`combat.escape-focus`).
- **Wait / bait** (`WAIT_BAIT_SCORE`): hold behind a screen so an un-acted enemy
  comes to you instead of you walking into it — a real timing tool.

**[IDEA — small]** Defend-vs-attack: in the packs, forgoing an available attack to
Defend was rare and usually correct (saving a body). One thing worth a look: a shooter
that Defends while it still has a **clean shot** is wasting a round. The code's attack
band already outranks Defend, so the AI should strike — I did **not** find a code gap
here; noting it only because you listed "defend" as a focus area.

## 7. When to fight / when to concede

- **Engage on your terms**: PvP is late (median R10). Don't initiate two hero-levels
  behind — `HERO_LEVEL_ENGAGE_MARGIN` already discounts engaging when the enemy main
  hero out-levels you [CODE].
- **Concede to save the war**: a truly lost fight should be conceded to preserve
  survivors (`PVP_CONCEDE_SCORE`, hopeless-retreat) [CODE]. **But** — per your note —
  a human give-up is **not** proof the board was lost (wa6ycw: p3 gave up the fight yet
  won the game). So the AI's concede must key on the **board state** (it does:
  `combatIsHopeless` + a real casualty), never on mimicking human concedes.
