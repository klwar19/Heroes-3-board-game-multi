# All town-unit experience progressions (anime and wuxia excluded)

Generated from the current unit definitions and live veterancy resolver. Neutral creatures, anime-town units, and MGQ/wuxia units are excluded. Stats and ability effects are written out in full rather than listed only by name.

## Experience thresholds

- Bronze: Seasoned 5 XP; Veteran 9 XP; Elite 13 XP; Legend 17 XP.
- Silver: Seasoned 6 XP; Veteran 10 XP; Elite 15 XP; Legend 20 XP.
- Gold/Azure: Seasoned 8 XP; Veteran 13 XP; Elite 19 XP; Legend 25 XP.

Included: **96 units across 13 towns** (Bulwark, Castle, Conflux, Cove, Dungeon, Factory, Fortress, Imperium, Inferno, Necropolis, Rampart, Stronghold, Tower).
