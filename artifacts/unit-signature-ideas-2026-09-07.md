# One signature per creature — 89 proposals

Design proposals, not implemented or playtested. These are candidates for replacing an existing R2, R3, or R4 reward, not 89 additions to current power. Date: 2026-09-07.

Scope confirmed by the user: the classic nine towns plus Cove, Factory, and Bulwark, using this project's unit entries. There are **89 entries**: 11 Conflux forms, 8 Factory units, and 7 in each other town. Few and Pack share a signature; the four extra basic elemental forms get separate signatures because the project models them separately. Neutral copies and custom factions are outside this file.

Read the [shared timing, token, movement, and balance rules](ability-ideas-general-2026-09-07.md) with these entries. U01–U89 are identifiers for discussion, not engine ability IDs. R2/R3/R4 are suggested unlock ranks. All new markers start empty; one charge means one charge per unit, not per Stack layer. A named secondary benefit cannot trigger from itself.

The [user's creature roster](https://mightandmagic.fandom.com/wiki/Heroes_III_creatures) anchors classic creature identities. The [HotA team's documentation](https://h3hota.com/en/documentation) anchors expansion identities. The invented abilities below are my interpretations of those identities, not canonical lore or official rules. Current board-game abilities and reward overlap were checked against the local unit definitions, ability registry, and experience schedule; PC abilities were not assumed to be board-game rules.

## How to choose among these

My strongest initial candidates are **U02 Split Volley, U07 Mercy Over Wrath, U12 Uprooting Grip, U32 Blood into Mist, U44 Worry the Flank, U53 Stone Bargain, U59 Static Link, U74 Shed the Venom, U75 Pocketed Luck, and U81 Shed the Mantle**. Each asks the player to exchange an existing advantage for something else.

**Module-dependent experiments:** U57/U62 need temporary terrain to exist, and U61/U83 depend on destructible obstacles. Do not assign them in a ruleset where their required objects never appear. The full general pool is deliberately broader than what should ship together.

Signatures can share small building blocks with the general pool. Their identity comes from the trigger, payment, and creature-specific interaction. Calling two different bonuses by lore names alone is not enough.

Before assigning any signature, inspect all four existing rank rewards. Replace conflicting rewards: do not stack a movement escape on top of every existing escape; do not award a second incompatible die replacement; do not let a payment trigger another ability. Conditional signatures must function on both Few and Pack, either directly or through the explicit fallback printed above.

The current generated schedule should remain the resolver. A future implementation should add approved per-unit signature overrides at the relevant rank, then regenerate the authoritative experience balance sheet. This proposal does not alter that schedule.
